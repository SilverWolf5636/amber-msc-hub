📖 Quick Guide

Help and tour: tap Menu, then Help, to replay the tour or read about one tab. A small ? next to a card title tells you what that card is for.

Saved on your phone: what you type is saved inside this browser on this device.

Home screen: in Safari or Chrome, tap Share / menu, then "Add to Home Screen".

Role switch: tap "Floor / Supervisor" before handing your phone to a lead. It hides the edit forms and delete buttons.

Text Task: on the Staff tab, type a task under a person and tap Text Task.

Sticky notes: the gold button makes notes you can drag around any tab.

Floor clock: follows the real time. Change it to practice a busy day, tap Live to go back.

Import a document: on the Docs tab, pick a Word, Excel, PDF, CSV or text file (or paste text). Tap "Auto-sort it for me" and the app guesses where each part belongs: showtimes, groups, contacts or steps. You see a preview first. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

Small header on phones: the "Store info" button shows or hides the phone chips and hours so your screen has more room.

Museum showtimes: a helper reads the public IMAX and Planetarium schedule once a day. On the Showtimes tab, pick a day and tap "Use these shows for my day". Your own shows are replaced, so the app asks you to tap twice.

Museum hours: a helper refreshes them once a day. The card shows when it last updated.
